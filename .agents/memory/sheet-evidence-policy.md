---
name: Carrier-sheet evidence policy
description: Why carrier matching stays conservative when the sheet lacks structured indication, route, and cargo fields.
---

Only explicitly named disease, administration route, and nucleic-acid cargo values should drive finder labels and results; a row-level text match is not proof that one formulation demonstrated all three together. Show partial matches with their gaps rather than inferring suitability or clinical effectiveness.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose literal extraction from the public sheet instead of inferred indications, routes, or cargos. Its scientific evidence is free text and includes reviews and limitations, so a strong recommendation would overstate what it supports.

**How to apply:** When extending matching or ranking, prefer exact sheet evidence and clear uncertainty. New metadata should come from the sheet itself, not a separate database or invented annotations.
