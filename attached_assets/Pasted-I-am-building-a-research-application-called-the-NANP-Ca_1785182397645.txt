One thing I'd change

There is one architectural improvement I'd make over this prompt.

Don't let GPT directly decide which SQL queries to run.

Instead:

User

↓

Backend

↓

Query Builder

↓

Supabase

↓

Retrieved Evidence

↓

GPT

↓

Final Answer

In other words, your backend owns the database access, and GPT only reasons over the evidence it receives.

This has three major benefits:

Security – GPT never has direct database credentials.
Reliability – You control exactly which records are retrieved.
Scalability – As your database grows from 38 papers to hundreds or thousands, you can improve retrieval (e.g., better ranking, embeddings, hybrid search) without changing the frontend or the LLM prompt.
